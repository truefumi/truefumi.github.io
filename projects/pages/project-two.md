# Networking Home Lab

**Summary:** designing and configuring a home lab for hosting services like Proxmox and OpenMediaVault for production-style use cases and Prometheus and Grafana for monitoring to simulate a professional production environment.

## Stack

`Language` · `Framework` · `Database` · `Hosting`

## Highlights

- This project was started to reduce monthly costs of paid subscription services while improving my networking skills and operation security at home
- I've decided to build all of my home lab out of materials that I've either found, scavenged, or bought for a significantly lower amount
- Learning by building is how I learn best. Building out my Home Lab allowed me to put all my "theoretical" knowledge into practice.
